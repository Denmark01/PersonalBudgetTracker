How entries are counted
Enter the total amount you paid for the full quantity. The app calculates the per-kg/litre/pack/piece rate automatically and uses the total price for all spending calculations.
For non-item transactions, choose EMI Payment or Savings / Investment to track fixed commitments separately from expenses, or choose Income, Credit Taken, Credit Paid, or Credit Card Due Paid. Use Credit Card as Pay type for card purchases; record bill repayments as Credit Card Due Paid so the outstanding card balance falls without counting the repayment as a second expense. These use one transaction amount and No unit automatically. EMI and savings/investment contributions reduce available cash but are excluded from expense totals. Salary and allocated budget are tracked separately. Credit taken and credit paid only update the outstanding credit balance; they do not change salary left or budget left.

Google Drive sync

ChatGPT JSON import · append transactions

Paste JSON generated by ChatGPT to APPEND new transactions to your existing tracker. This never replaces your current data, salary, budgets or settings. Review the preview first; possible duplicates are skipped.
Required: date (YYYY-MM-DD), name, amount. Optional: category, entryType, payType, paidBy, familyMember, subItem, qty, unit. Amount is the total amount paid. Maximum 500 transactions per import.

Backup & recovery

Download Full Backup saves all transactions, months, salary, budgets, category limits, savings targets and tracker settings. Upload Backup File performs a FULL RESTORE and replaces the current tracker data. ChatGPT JSON import only APPENDS transactions. Before restoring, the app downloads a safety backup of your current data and asks for confirmation. Item prices are saved as total prices; unit rates are calculated automatically.
